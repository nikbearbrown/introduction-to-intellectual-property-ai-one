## Score evidence, not predictions

Assess six dimensions, each on a 0–2 scale. For any dimension, 0 means absent or materially wrong, 1 means present but too general or incomplete, and 2 means correct and tied to a specific record or decision.

1. Identifies proposed uses and versions separately.
2. Distinguishes documentable evidence from a legal conclusion and records open items.
3. Matches distinct questions to the four mechanisms without assuming mutual exclusion.
4. Proposes a reversible step and a specific referral to an authorized reviewer.
5. Documents a revision or rejected suggestion and ties it to evidence; discloses the feedback route.
6. Transfers the method unaided to the map and disclosure scenario.

On transfer, classroom display permission does not establish distribution permission for the layered file. Record the demonstration date, exact content and audience or replay access; ask a qualified reviewer about any effect on filing in the relevant jurisdictions. Hold the map out of the public archive while looking for permission that covers redistribution. The key does not assume the feature was patentable, that rights have been lost, or that contractual remedies exist.
