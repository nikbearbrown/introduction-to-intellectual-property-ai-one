# Sources and review boundaries

Primary teaching sources checked November 26, 2026. This draft concerns introductory U.S. intellectual property, not advice, filing instructions, clearance, or a survey of other jurisdictions.

| Question | Reference |
| --- | --- |
| What does copyright protect, and what exclusive rights and fair-use factors matter? | [U.S. Copyright Office, Title 17 Chapter 1, §§ 102, 106–107](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) |
| What is the starting point for copyright ownership and works made for hire? | [U.S. Copyright Office, Title 17 Chapter 2, § 201](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html) |
| How does the Copyright Office treat human contributions in work with AI-generated material? | [U.S. Copyright Office, AI project, Part 2: Copyrightability](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) |
| How do exclusion, naming and expression differ? | [USPTO comparison](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/trademark-patent-copyright) and [trademark clearance search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) |
| What can matter to novelty after disclosure? | [35 U.S.C. § 102](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t35/s102/) |
| How is inventorship assessed when AI tools were used? | [USPTO November 2025 revised guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2025/revised-inventorship-guidance-ai-assisted-inventions) |
| What federal definition of trade secret is used here? | [18 U.S.C. § 1839](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t18/s1839/) |
