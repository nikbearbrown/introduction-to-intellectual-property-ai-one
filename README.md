# Introduction to Intellectual Property (AI Plus One)

## About the book

A team is ready to share a campus equipment checkout app. Can it post a copied function, use its proposed name and film its demo? This compact **rough draft** teaches business and design students how to use U.S. intellectual property concepts to identify those questions, follow the evidence and hand unresolved decisions to the right reviewer. No legal training is assumed. It is introductory education, not legal advice, and has not had a disciplinary expert edit.

## Start reading

Read [Inventory the contributions](chapters/01-inventory-the-contributions.md), then follow the [complete chapter index](chapters/README.md). The Markdown here is the reading copy. No separate site or formatted download is available in this draft.

## Chapter guide

**Part I: Recognize and record**

1. [Inventory the contributions](chapters/01-inventory-the-contributions.md). Create a version-specific register for code, images and names.
2. [Compare the protections](chapters/02-compare-the-protections.md). Separate copyright, patent, trademark and secrecy questions.
3. [Read the permissions](chapters/03-read-the-permissions.md). Check whether terms cover viewing, adapting or distributing a particular artifact.

**Part II: Decide what needs review**

4. [Trace collaboration and disclosure](chapters/04-trace-collaboration-and-disclosure.md). Reconstruct contributions and what different audiences can see.
5. [Write the review handoff](chapters/05-write-the-review-handoff.md). State the decision, missing facts and what to refer.

## How to teach or study it

The same fictional launch runs through all five chapters. First inventory the assets, then classify the questions and follow the evidence through two different releases. Complete the [dossier](practice/release-dossier.md) before reading the [key](practice/answer-key.md), revise with paper or optional model feedback, then do the transfer item unaided. The [teaching notes](teaching/README.md) describe one proposed sequence and link the scope and sources.
